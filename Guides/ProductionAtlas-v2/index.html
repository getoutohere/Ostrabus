Start at a station and choose a good it can produce. The left column shows other stations with recipes that consume that good. Choose a station, then an output branch to see the exact recipe and any co-inputs.

Continue with this output carries the full calculated output quantity into the next leg. Use the chain breadcrumbs to go back. You are exploring one alternative at a time, not allocating cargo across every branch.

Net surplus
Gross production minus all local recipe consumption at their full configured rates. This is a theoretical balance, not actual inventory.
All output
Includes goods produced locally even if local recipes would consume them. Exporting these may compete with local production.
Shipment batches
Only whole recipe cycles run. All resulting output carries forward; leftovers remain at the receiving port. Configured cycle rates determine processing time, not a limit on total output. Other ingredients are supplied separately, and storage is cleared as needed. Times exclude travel and game scheduling delays.
Cargo capacity
Each pod holds 1,000 kg. Whole units are packed per pod using an estimated category mass; the initial mass is editable. Onward pod counts cover output only, excluding co-inputs and leftovers. Larger outputs are never silently discarded: use more capacity or multiple trips.
Knock-on effects
Later legs show what the newly produced goods could enable elsewhere. They do not include existing production at the receiving station or prove a profitable route.
