READ THE MAP

Follow a supply chain,
not a price signal.

Start at a station and choose a good it can produce. The left column shows other stations with recipes that consume that good. Choose a station, then an output branch to see the exact recipe and any co-inputs.

Continue with this output carries the calculated output rate into the next leg. Use the chain breadcrumbs to go back. You are exploring one alternative at a time, not allocating cargo across every branch.

Net surplus
Gross production minus all local recipe consumption at their full configured rates. This is a theoretical balance, not actual inventory.
All output
Includes goods produced locally even if local recipes would consume them. Exporting these may compete with local production.
Rates
Category units per game hour, not tonnes. A branch is capped by your incoming rate and the recipe's cycle rate; all other ingredients are assumed available. Stored stock and transport are not modeled.
Knock-on effects
Later legs show what the newly produced goods could enable elsewhere. They do not include existing production at the receiving station or prove a profitable route.

Some installed recipe names are misleading. This app uses actual input/output fields: notably, OKLG’s recipe named “ToHVAC” produces Control Systems.
