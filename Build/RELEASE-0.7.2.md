# Ostrabus 0.7.2

Prepared for **Ostranauts 1.1.0.4 (`public_test`)**, Windows and BepInEx 5.
Status remains experimental and partially playtested. Full deployment still
installs the complete main plugin and its four bundled native mods; standalone
plugins retain their own versions and installers. Preparation does not deploy,
commit, tag, push or publish.

## Changes since 0.7.1

- **Game 1.1.0.4 support:** review the native code and asset changes, retire the
  PDC conversion-ammunition hook now fixed by native weapon recipes, and retain
  the historical custom-build ID as a no-op for existing profiles; see the
  [compatibility review](COMPATIBILITY-1.1.0.4.md).
- **AI hull avoidance:** confirm broad ship-collision warnings against cached
  hull exteriors so oversized prediction circles alone do not cause evasive
  maneuvers; retain conservative fallback when geometry or future motion cannot
  be established, without changing native docking clearance or weapon hits.
  See [the investigation and playtest limits](../Navigation/PLANETARY-AVOIDANCE-AUDIT.md).
- **Derelict names:** preserve the name generated by `Ship.InitShip` when
  `EnvironmentEntities.SpawnShipFromStellarObject` materializes a deferred wreck,
  instead of replacing it with its callsign.
- **Ship registrations:** make `Ship.GenerateID` reject IDs already held by
  deferred space objects as well as loaded ships, retaining the caller's prefix
  on retries; explicit-ID restoration and conversion keep their existing identity.
  Both identity repairs report interventions in the native player console and
  are independently selectable in custom builds. See the
  [native reproductions and scope](../MiscBugFixes/DERELICT-NAMES-AND-IDS-AUDIT.md).
- **Wreck-bonus diagnostics:** extend the existing optional damage-log setting
  to report native bonus awards, their Beat Manager or interaction source, and
  subsequent consumption; the observer does not alter bonus awards, random
  draws or the configured severity curve. See
  [logging and balance interpretation](../QoL/WreckSeverity/README.md).
- **Tools and evidence:** retain the read-only
  [saved-world retention study](../Tools/SaveBloatStudy/README.md), updated native
  conversion inspection and the ship-executive research
  [postmortem](../Navigation/Architecture/POSTMORTEM.md). No replacement AI
  controller or automatic save cleanup is introduced.

## Independently installed plugins

- **[Bottle Refiller 0.1.7](../StandaloneMods/BottleRefiller/README.md)** is new:
  powered wall equipment fills up to four handheld O2 bottles from a nearby tank
  or compartment, conserving each gas species and retaining its mixture. Empty
  bottles reach 90% rated pressure after five game seconds and full after thirty
  when supply permits. Native controls, work conversions and inventory are used;
  the plugin services loaded equipment through one command-loader hook.
  It has separate healthy/damaged and installed/loose artwork, filling/idle
  indicators, audio, and a stock-composing installer. It is not an oxygen filter
  and does not require the main plugin. See its
  [validation and remaining scene checks](../StandaloneMods/BottleRefiller/VALIDATION.md).
- **[Boneyard Turnover 0.1.5](../StandaloneMods/BoneyardTurnover/README.md)**
  accepts the reviewed 1.1.0.4 assembly while retaining its turnover policy,
  protections and saved state. Existing installations need this separate update;
  main deployment does not replace its DLL.
- **[Station Kiosk Migration 0.1.4](../StandaloneMods/StationMigration/README.md)**
  accepts the reviewed loader and reads native named weapon-uninstallation
  outputs, preserving an obstructing weapon's damage and ammunition. Already
  migrated saves do not need another migration.
- **[Apartment Refuel Terminal 0.1.1](../StandaloneMods/ApartmentRefuel/README.md)**
  retains its independent version and receives the current native compatibility
  label. Its separate installer remains included in the main archive without
  activation.

## Evidence and remaining limits

Read-only live inspection confirmed the reviewed game assembly and successful
plugin startup, consistent fleet/market snapshots, actual departure of a loaded
OKLG freighter, and a native bonus award following a failed story-event attempt.
The latter confirms the bonus observer runs; it is not a change to story policy.
The freighter departure does not establish its eventual delivery.

The hull correction passes native geometry/approach tests, but the first live
pursuit still showed disruptive evasion and replanning. Subsequent analysis
distinguished a false-circle warning from genuine projected hull contact; the
player chose to retain resisting-target behavior and the geometry correction.
No manual-clearance exemption was added. Offline clamp tests are not a complete
forced-docking flight replay.

The two identity repairs prevent new defects; they do not rename existing
callsign-only wrecks, restore overwritten objects, or establish the cause of an
unavailable reporter's captain/docking incident. Bottle Refiller has basic live
operation and exported-art approval, with full scene appearance, audible output,
damage/repair transitions and save reconstruction still incompletely accepted.

Native integration fixtures substitute documented Unity scene boundaries.
Assertions from numerical sweeps are not separate campaign playtests. Previous
PASS-loading, historical-casualty and reload-memory limitations remain documented
in [0.7.1](RELEASE-0.7.1.md); this release does not claim to resolve them.

outside source control. Release checks write only workspace fixtures and do not
deploy to the running game or change player saves.
