Fuel and tank budget

Assumes extra fuel needs new tanks, with no spare space in existing tanks. Each new tank adds 1,500 kg to ship mass; fuel contents add nothing. Full capacities: 5,216 kg He-3 and 44,722.8 kg D2O per tank. Changing existing tank counts estimates availability without adding their hardware mass again.

What the game actually calculates

The thermal ceiling balances heat removed by CYCLE against the maximum feeder/laser heating rate. Cryopumps remove excess heat above the ideal temperature; they do not rescue a core that cannot generate enough heat. Capacitors support at most two lasers each, and regulators support at most two feeders each.

Plotted-course fuel is based on rated thrust, powered time and the pilot efficiency modifier. It is not proportional to the selected G setting. Manual fusion instead consumes according to actual pellet rate and core temperature. The manual estimate assumes an ideal-temperature equilibrium; the plotted estimate is the appropriate route budget.

Assumes an ignited reactor in thrust mode, forward containment enabled, adequate power, cryogen supply when cryopumps are entered, and functional supporting equipment. Initial core temperature is ideal. Core/coil faults, startup, idle burn, gradual component wear, acceleration-induced hull damage and complete trip geometry are outside this calculator. Mass is the entered current total; values do not integrate changing mass over a journey. No live game connection or game-state writes.

Formulas traced to FusionIC.Run / Fusion / MassUsageMax, Ship.GetMaxTorchThrust / CalculateTorchFuelConsumption, and NavData.TimeAdvance / SetReactants. See the accompanying README for validation and limits.
