Keep the white circular disc, centered composition, generous margin and bold black eight-tooth gear silhouette. Replace the gear's empty central circular hole with a clearly readable miniature control-panel symbol: three simple horizontal white slider tracks with staggered small white rectangular knobs, inside the black gear center. Enlarge the central solid black area enough to keep these sliders readable at 64 pixels. Flat pure black and white, crisp edges with light antialiasing only. No text, letters, shading, gradients, glow, texture, perspective, extra badges, or mockup. Outside the white disc is genuine alpha transparency; the disc and black glyph remain opaque. Square canvas, ideally matching the references' 256 by 256 pixel dimensions. Produce one finished icon.
