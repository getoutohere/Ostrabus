# RCS module comparison

| Characteristic | Testudo S-Lift | Havlorson Washburne | Ryokka CL-03 | Miura Chimera |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| **Actual thrust** | **100%** | **135%** | **218.75%** | **250%** |
| Full-throttle fuel consumption | 100% | 150% | 175% | 125% |
| **Fuel for the same maneuver** | **100%** | **111%** | **80%** | **50%** |
| Module mass | 28 kg | 19 kg | 51.5 kg | 16.5 kg |
| Powered electrical draw | ~10 W | ~7.3 W | ~15 W | ~28 W |
| Maximum damage capacity | 6 | 6 | 15 | 4 |
| Mounting | Standard | Standard | Double-width | Corner mount |
| Base price | $3,864 | $5,520 | $8,140 | $12,370 |

Performance comparisons assume equal module counts and total ship mass, healthy
powered modules, default fuel-efficiency modifiers, and uniform thruster types.
Base prices are asset values; actual market prices vary.

- **Washburne:** lighter and stronger, but spends more fuel for the same maneuver.
- **Ryokka:** heavy, bulky and durable; considerably stronger and more efficient.
- **Chimera:** strongest, lightest and most fuel-efficient, with fragile hardware,
  awkward mounting and expensive repairs.

Native thrust multiplies strength by exhaust efficiency. The asset multipliers
are:

| Multiplier | Testudo | Washburne | Ryokka | Chimera |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Strength | 1.0 | 1.5 | 1.75 | 1.25 |
| Exhaust | 1.0 | 0.9 | 1.25 | 2.0 |
