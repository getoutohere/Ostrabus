# Round proximity knob

Generated with the built-in image_gen tool. `RoundKnob.png` is the original
transparent output, embedded in the plugin DLL and rotated in Unity. Numbers and
detent placement are drawn separately by the UI. No offline raster rotation or
image alteration is performed.

## Prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset: one small rotary knob sprite for an industrial spacecraft navigation console UI. Strict orthographic top-down view, perfectly circular centered dark charcoal metal knob with evenly spaced small finger-grip ridges all around its circumference. A single bold burnt-orange radial index line runs from near the center toward the twelve-o'clock edge, stopping just inside the rim. The orange line must remain clearly readable when the entire knob is displayed only 24 pixels wide. Restrained flat game UI rendering with subtle concentric bevels and rotationally symmetric ambient lighting, no directional cast shadow. Circular body occupies about 84 percent of a square canvas, evenly centered, with fully transparent background and generous clean transparent padding. No numbers, letters, tick marks, labels, plate, screws, extra controls, scenery, reflections, extra dots or extra index lines. One finished sprite, not a sheet. Prefer 256 by 256 pixels. Smooth clean silhouette and alpha edge, neutral dark gray metal and muted safety orange.
